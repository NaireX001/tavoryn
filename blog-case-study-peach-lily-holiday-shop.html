Case study: The Peach & Lily Holiday Shop

Doc D · TAVORYNNovember 19, 20265 min read

How we turned 44 holiday products into an easy, giftable shopping experience on Shopify.

TAVORYN designed and built The Holiday Shop for Peach & Lily, the Korean skincare brand behind Glass Skin. It's a seasonal gift destination on their Shopify store that turns 44 holiday products into an easy, giftable shopping experience.

The brief and the challenge

Holiday shoppers aren't buying for themselves. They're buying for someone else, often with a budget in mind and little skincare knowledge. A standard product grid of serums and creams asks them to do too much work.

The challenge was to take a large seasonal range and present it so any gift-giver could find the right thing quickly. It also had to feel unmistakably Peach & Lily: warm, joyful and premium, in the brand's signature coral and pink.

The goals

Make the season feel special from the first scroll. Help shoppers choose fast by grouping gifts by type and price, not by skincare category. Put the best-value holiday kits and their savings front and center. Give undecided shoppers an easy way out with a digital gift card. And work smoothly on mobile, where most holiday browsing happens.

Design approach

A soft pink, bokeh-lit backdrop and coral ribbon wrapping the products set a festive tone without leaving the brand palette. The headline "The Holiday Shop" sits in Peach & Lily's elegant serif, with a short line, "Skincare worth wrapping", that frames every product as a gift.

The page leads shoppers from inspiration to the right gift in a few steps: a festive hero with one "Shop Now" button; three entry tiles for Kits, Under $25 and Under $50; a gift grid of all 44 products with filters, "Selling Fast" badges, ratings and clear sale prices; a "Give the Gift of Glass Skin" carousel that matches gifts to personalities; and a closing digital gift card banner.

Shopping by recipient and budget removes the hardest question for a gift-giver: "what do they actually need?"

Building it on Shopify

The Holiday Shop is a Shopify collection linked to sub-collections (Holiday Kits, Gifts Under $25, Gifts Under $50), so new products drop into the right place automatically. Sale and compare-at pricing, product badges and review stars appear on every card, with quick add-to-bag and size options without leaving the page.

Shoppers can filter and sort all 44 products, with pagination keeping the page fast. The hero, tiles, grid and carousels reflow for phone, tablet and desktop, with touch-friendly carousels on mobile. The page is reachable from the homepage hero and the "Holiday Collections" section, so seasonal traffic flows straight in.

The result

The Holiday Shop is live at peachandlily.com/collections/holiday-shop and anchors Peach & Lily's seasonal campaign from the homepage.

Seasonal campaigns live or die on how easy they make it to buy. TAVORYN designs and builds Shopify pages that turn browsers into buyers.
